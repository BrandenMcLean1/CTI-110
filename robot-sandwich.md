# Sandwich Construction

## Assumptions
- We have an unobstructed marble island
- On the marble island we have:
   - a plate with two slices of bread side-by-side
   - jar of peanut butter
   - jar of jelly
   - a butter knife
   - damp towel
- the robot is charged and ready to go
<br><br><br>

## Steps to build the sandwich
1. Twist the lid on the jar of peanut butter open to the left 
2. Twist the lid on the jar of jelly open to the left
3. Grab and pick up the butter knife
4. Scoop peanut butter from jar with butter knife in hand
5. Spread peanut butter on right slice of bread
6. Use damp towel to clean knife
7. Use the butter knife to scoop jelly from jar
8. Spread jelly on left slice of bread
9. Use damp towel to clean knife
10. Discard the damp towel
11. Place butter knife down
12. Place left slice of bread on top of right slice of bread
13. Put lids back on peanut butter and jelly
14. Put peanut butter, jelly, knife away

<br><br><br>

## Exit Conditions
- Plate and constructed sandwich are only items on the marble island
- Put plate in sink 
- Put untensils in sink
- Put jar of both peanut butter and jelly back in storage
